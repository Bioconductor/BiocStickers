# The `RJMCMCNucleosomes` sticker

Sticker of the [RJMCMCNucleosomes](https://github.com/adeschen/RJMCMCNucleosomes)
R package, which detects nucleosome positions using a Bayesian hierarchical 
model and a reversible jump Markov chain Monte Carlo (RJMCMC) algorithm.

The sticker shows a nucleosome, the structure detected by [RJMCMCNucleosomes](https://github.com/adeschen/RJMCMCNucleosomes):

* the eight balls are the histone octamer, drawn as a 2 x 2 x 2 block; the
  four shades of orange stand for the four pairs of histones (H2A, H2B, H3
  and H4);
* the light blue line is the DNA, wrapped about two turns around the
  octamer: each loop passes behind the octamer at the top and in front of it
  at the bottom;
* the two short ends are the linker DNA that connects the nucleosome to its
  neighbors.
